Our Story

Meet Your Chef, Jean Smith

Jean Smith is the heart and soul behind Sweet Momma's — a family-run kitchen in Norwich, Connecticut bringing authentic southern cooking and pastry artistry to New England.

A graduate of The Connecticut Culinary Institute, Jean holds a pastry certificate and brings four decades of experience to every dish. Her cooking has been featured in the Norwich Bulletin and beloved by the community since 2020.
